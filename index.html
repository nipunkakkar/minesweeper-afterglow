A QUICK REFRESHER

One square at a time.

Uncover every safe square without hitting a mine. The numbers tell you how many of the eight surrounding squares contain mines.

  • Uncover: click or tap. Your first opening and its neighbors are always safe.
  • Flag: right-click, hold for half a second, or choose Flag mode. Tap again to remove a flag.
  • Clear around a number: click an open number after placing the right number of flags around it. Incorrect flags can trigger a mine!
  • Sonar: activate it, then pick any square. The outlined area contains the reported number of mines, including any you have flagged. Edges have smaller scan areas. Each pulse adds 10 seconds.
  • Keyboard: arrows move; Enter uncovers; F or Space flags; S readies sonar; Escape cancels sonar. P pauses.

The clock pauses when you switch tabs or open these instructions. Best times stay in this browser, with separate sonar and unassisted records. Retro changes the look; the sonar surprise stays.
